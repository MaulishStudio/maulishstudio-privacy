Privacy Policy

Bubble magician's journey

Effective date: 9 October 2026

MaulishStudio develops Bubble magician's journey, an Android game identified by com.maulish.bubblewitch. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, view advertisements, make purchases, or contact us.

Privacy contact: maulish.studio@proton.me

Information saved on your device

The game stores your best distance, selected cosmetic skins, purchased product identifiers, and transaction identifiers locally to remember your progress and unlocks. Purchase receipts are checked on your device to verify purchases. MaulishStudio does not operate a server for uploading your gameplay saves.

The game does not provide an account registration or login system. It does not ask you to enter your name, email address, password, or payment card details.

Advertisements

The game uses Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads to load and display rewarded and interstitial advertisements. These services and their advertising partners collect and process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, where available, and technical details about your device and the app.
  • Network information, including an IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Ad impressions, views, taps, and other interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic information about the operation of the advertising services.

Providers use this information to deliver and measure ads, perform analytics, support their services, and prevent fraud. They may share it with advertising partners and service providers for these purposes. Advertising personalization depends on the provider, your region, and applicable privacy choices. This processing is described in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases of cosmetic items use Google Play Billing through Unity In-App Purchasing. Google processes payment information under its own terms. MaulishStudio does not receive your payment card or bank account details.

The game receives purchase receipts, product identifiers, transaction identifiers, and purchase status to verify, deliver, and restore items. Unity IAP may process purchase history, installation and session identifiers, device information, and approximate country information for purchasing, reporting, and diagnostics. The current game uses Google Play's purchase flow.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for the providers' practices.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as a description of a problem or purchase reference. We use this information to respond, resolve the issue, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Sharing and security

Information may be processed by Google and Unity as described above and by our email provider when you contact us. We may disclose information where necessary to comply with law or protect users and our services. MaulishStudio does not sell support correspondence.

We use platform billing, local receipt verification, and access restrictions on support correspondence. Unity describes its advertising and purchasing services as encrypting transmitted data. Information held by third parties is subject to their security measures and privacy policies. No storage or transmission method guarantees complete security.

Retention and deletion

Local saves and purchase caches remain until you clear the game's app data or remove them from your device. Android backup or restore features may retain or restore copies according to your device settings. Clearing local data does not cancel purchases or delete records held by Google or Unity.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve your request, meet legal obligations, or handle disputes. You may request deletion by emailing us. Google and Unity retain information they process according to their own policies and applicable obligations.

For information held by MaulishStudio, contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. We will handle requests as applicable and help direct requests concerning provider-held data. Where Unity processes information on our behalf, we will work with Unity on the request. Records may need to be retained for legal, security, or transaction purposes.

Your choices

You can clear local game data using Android's app storage settings. Android may also provide controls to reset or delete your advertising ID. Provider privacy options are available through their policies linked above. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent advertising services from processing information when they initialize or load other ads.

Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including rights to object to or restrict certain processing. Email us with questions or requests. Advertising and purchasing providers may process information outside your country; their policies describe applicable transfer protections.

Younger players

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game or in a message to us, contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate steps with the relevant providers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
